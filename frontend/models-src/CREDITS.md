# 3D model credits

`public/models/desk.glb` is generated by `build_desk.py` (with `airplane.py`).
The notebook, the page rig, the handwriting and the paper airplane are made
in those scripts. One part comes from elsewhere:

- **Fountain pen** — "Fountain Pens" by kirikom9000, licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fountain-pens-48de5fd3726d4792a474cb9781549155
  Changes: one of the two pens kept, re-seated with its origin at the nib,
  textures reduced to 512 px, the black lacquer recoloured violet, and the
  cap animated (uncapping and posting).
